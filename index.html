Parent’s moderator guide

  1. Select a 30-question round and read each question aloud from the start.
  2. Have Abhinav buzz verbally or with a desk buzzer when he recognizes the answer. Stop reading immediately at the buzz.
  3. Reveal the answer and mark Correct, Prompted, or Missed. Save brief notes on confused names or map locations.
  4. Revisit Missed questions next session, ideally with an atlas. Track scores over multiple days rather than attempting all 360 in one sitting.

Study sources and accuracy notes

Official: 2026–27 White Set Geography Study Guide, International Academic Competitions; IAC study guide index. Each official guide is a starting list, not the entire exam syllabus. Additional: Abhinav’s September parent geography practice pack (110 questions); Ram Iyer’s October–November country-study assignments and materials (topics expanded into newly authored questions); user-supplied 78-question White draft or 35-question original bank, as applicable; saved GeoBeeTrainer question bank. Source/focus labels are shown on every card.

Corrections applied: Djenné and adjacent Djenné-Djeno are distinct historical locations; Erbil is not situated directly on the Greater Zab; the city of Santiago is not treated as on the Azuero Peninsula; phrases such as “most remote city” and “four times larger” were softened where rankings vary. Some older source packets and recordings were inaccessible; no unreviewed contents have been presented as extracted facts. Geographical definitions, transliterations and elevation figures can vary; accept clearly equivalent names.

Study priorities: locations on a blank map; rivers and drainage basins; mountain systems and plate boundaries; islands, straits, coasts and chokepoints; cities, capitals, and landmarks; physical/human/economic geography; Canadian provinces and landforms; Ram’s current country studies. The source tag “authored clues” means a new practice question written around a published guide topic, not official IAC question text.

Designed for offline personal study. Use the relevant published guide as the authoritative starting point; this compilation has not been individually verified as an official tournament question bank.
