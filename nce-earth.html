About these papers

These are the US National Science Championship Exam (NCE) Earth Science papers from Coach Ram's "time permitting" pack (2020–2024 US National Science Bee materials) — Ram flagged them as Science Bee cross-over: earth science and astronomy are a full strand of the IAC Science Bee syllabus, and these papers drill exactly that strand at Elementary, Middle School and Varsity/JV levels.

Format: 75 multiple-choice questions per paper, official scoring +2 correct / −1 wrong / 0 blank. Exam mode here runs a 50-minute practice clock (40 seconds a question — train pace, not just knowledge). In Study mode every answer checks instantly.

Blank discipline: with −1 for a wrong answer, a blind guess among four options loses points on average (−0.25). Guess only when you can eliminate it down to two.

How to use: start with the Middle School paper in Study mode, then sit the Varsity/JV paper in Exam mode. The Elementary paper is the warm-up / confidence set.

Real exam scoring: correct +2, wrong -1, left blank 0. In Exam mode the clock runs 25:00 and answers are only revealed when you submit. Your best Exam-mode score per set is saved on this device.
